Rules and limits
  • Price priority: buyers with higher accepted prices go first; select the cheapest eligible supplier. Trades settle at the supplier price.
  • Balanced service: prioritize buyers with fewer fulfilled units; rotate ties by round; select the cheapest eligible supplier.
  • Dispersed purchasing: use balanced buyer ordering, but prioritize eligible suppliers with fewer cumulative sales, then lower price.
  • Budgets do not reset between rounds. Supply capacity resets; unfilled demand does not carry forward. No learning, negotiation, real payments or model calls. Results describe this model only.

What counts as evidence?

  1. Include all 27 runs for a full experiment package. For a correction, include the smallest reproducible case instead.
  2. Record the WORLD version, seeds, scenarios, rule names and original exports. Attach files to the GitHub issue before submitting.
  3. Explain at least one trade-off and a limitation. Report unexpected or unfavorable outcomes too.
  4. Disclose author/reviewer relationships. An author cannot approve their own award. Rejection or disagreement must include reasons.

A complete independent review may confirm or disprove the original finding. Agreement is not a condition for recognition.
