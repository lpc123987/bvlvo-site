Current status

BVLVO has not issued a token. There is no official token contract, public network, production deployment, sale, purchase route, market, exchange listing or promised launch date.

The project is currently researching whether useful AI work can be described, performed by an unfamiliar machine, measured, verified and settled correctly. Distributed AI batch jobs are the first proposed test scenario.

No date shown in an archived document should be interpreted as a current issuance, deployment or trading commitment.

Research limits

The homepage presents a long-term thesis. It does not establish that the proposed protocol is technically feasible, secure, economically sustainable, legally available or capable of attracting real demand.

  • A task payment does not prove the task result is correct.
  • A token-allocation test does not prove useful machine work.
  • A successful testnet transaction does not establish production readiness.
  • Reputation, verification, dispute handling and owner authority remain research problems.
  • No independent security audit has been completed for a production system.

Historical material

Earlier pages describe token design, governance, treasury controls, issuance planning and testnet prototypes. They remain available as a transparent engineering archive, but they have been superseded by the Personal AI Economy and Machine Labor Economy direction.

Archived figures, dates, allocations and mechanisms are not current commitments. They do not create token rights, payment obligations, governance authority or an entitlement to future distributions.

Security & authenticity

Only information published on bvlvo.com, the linked public repository and the official @BVLVOLabs X account should be treated as project communication. The existence of a similarly named token, address, pool, account or website does not make it official.

BVLVO will never ask for a password, seed phrase, private key or verification code. No payment, deposit or wallet signature is required to read the research materials.

Website data

The public website is primarily informational. Do not submit private identity records, confidential business data, model secrets, customer files, wallet secrets or recovery information through public repository issues or social media.

Future work-market designs must distinguish public verification evidence from sensitive task inputs. Customer files, prompts, personal data and embeddings should not be placed indiscriminately on a public blockchain.
