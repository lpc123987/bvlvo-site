Planned issuance & jurisdiction

BVLVO has not announced or deployed an official token contract. No sale, subscription, deposit collection, redemption or official trading facility is available through this website. Publishing a proposal does not establish authorization to issue or market a token.

Responsible issuing arrangements and territorial eligibility remain unresolved. International website access is not an invitation or confirmation of eligibility to acquire a token. Applicable requirements can depend on actual operation, control, audience and activity; overseas hosting alone does not resolve them.

China’s 6 February 2026 notice (Yin Fa [2026] No. 42), among other provisions, restricts domestic token-related business and overseas virtual-currency issuance by domestic entities and their controlled overseas entities without the required consent. The project has not established an applicable permitted issuance route.

Material uncertainties

Any eventual token could lose substantial or all market value, have little or no liquidity, or fail to attract users. Allocation releases and concentrated holdings can create pressure and control risks. No amount of reporting guarantees a buyer, a price or a return.

Contract defects, wallet compromise, infrastructure failures, regulatory changes and team discontinuity could interrupt or terminate the project. An audit, if later commissioned, would not eliminate these risks.

Website & public submissions

The current informational pages do not request wallet signatures or collect payments. The task form prepares a GitHub draft in the browser; creating the issue happens on GitHub. Submitted issue text, account identity and attachments may be public. Do not submit secrets, identity documents or private customer data.

Hosting, network and linked services may process connection information and maintain their own logs or cookies under their policies. This notice does not promise anonymous browsing or zero data processing. Any future account, wallet or analytics feature needs an updated notice before activation.

Official references & impersonation

The project website is bvlvo.com, the linked X account is @BVLVOLabs and the public repository is lpc123987/bvlvo-site. No audit partner, investor, exchange endorsement or named executive team is claimed. Never send a seed phrase or private key to anyone claiming to represent the project.

Contact and corrections currently use public GitHub issues. A private support address and formal responsible-operator details have not been published. A proposal on this site is not a contract granting an allocation or future payout.
