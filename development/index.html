BVLVO / Research & development

From issuance infrastructure to useful machine work.

BVLVO is moving from a token-first prototype toward the economic protocol for personal AI and machine labor. Historical contract and testnet work remains public, but it is no longer the product roadmap.

Current / protocol researchNo token issuedNo production network

Current direction

Prove useful work before designing expansion.

Persistent identity

Define how a human-owned AI remains itself across models, devices and physical or digital forms.

Verifiable work

Describe the task, the result and the verification outcome before any economic settlement occurs.

Earned reputation

Keep reputation separate from transferable assets. It must be earned through real work, not purchased.

First protocol proof

Can one unfamiliar machine complete a useful AI job for another party — and have the work measured, verified and settled correctly?

Starting scenario

Distributed AI batch jobs

Batch inference, embeddings, transcription, image classification and other divisible non-real-time work.
