The BVLVO token

A proposed transferable community token. Supply, allocation, rights and unresolved issuance decisions.

Purpose before issuance

BVLVO is proposed as an onchain community token for long-term participation and publicly accountable project development. Potential uses include formal proposals, bounded community decisions and budgeted recognition of accepted contributions. These functions are not live.

A community thesis is not an asset backing. BVLVO currently has no demonstrated recurring service demand, redemption mechanism or established independent governance network. Transferability alone does not establish utility, market demand or value.

Supply & allocation design

The issuance design sets a maximum of 1,000,000,000 BVLVO. This is a denomination choice, not a valuation. None of the allocations below has been issued, granted or enforced onchain.

The issuance design generates the full supply once and retains no further minting capability. This is an implementation objective, not a verified property of an existing contract. Chain, token standard, decimals and final contract architecture remain undecided.

Release rules

The team release design sets a 12-month cliff followed by 48 equal monthly releases: 3,125,000 tokens per month, beginning in month 13 after an eventual issuance. No real vesting contract or entitlement exists today.

The design releases other pools only against disclosed budgets and accepted work. Unused allocations remain unspent; a ten-year planning horizon does not require exhausting them. Work must not earn duplicate awards across pools.

A reserve label is not a lock. Before issuance, publish the controllers, associated parties, transfer restrictions and release schedule for every pool. Initial circulation cannot be calculated until those arrangements are complete.

Rights & limits

Holding BVLVO would not by itself confer company equity, dividends, a claim on treasury assets, access to private user data or a right to override an agreed contract. Any future participation rights must be specified before activation.

There is no official purchase route, token contract, sale price or confirmed exchange listing. A project token would also not automatically pay the underlying blockchain’s network fees.

Trading & liquidity

Trading is a separate decision from creating a token. A liquidity allocation does not supply the paired asset needed for a market. Funding source, position ownership, withdrawal powers and trade-size stress tests must be disclosed before any trading arrangement.

The issuance design excludes transfer taxes, fixed yield, guaranteed repurchases and price support promises. Fees, volatility, low depth and changing supply can cause substantial losses. No burn mechanism or revenue-linked purchase programme has been adopted.
