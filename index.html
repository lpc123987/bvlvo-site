05 / QUESTIONS

Before you participate.

Has BVLVO been issued?

No. There is no official contract, sale or purchase route. Designed supply figures are not circulating tokens.

What would holders be able to do?

Formal proposals and bounded community decisions are under study. Voting thresholds, eligible balances and execution arrangements are not finalized or active.

Does the ten-year vision guarantee value?

No. It describes an intended planning horizon. Neither sustained funding, market demand, trading liquidity nor a token price is guaranteed.

What happened to WORLD?

WORLD and Task 001 remain available as early experiments. They are deterministic simulations and public research records, not evidence of token income or real AI service delivery.

Who operates the project?

The project is in founder-led preparation. A formal responsible issuer, named governance signers and a funded operating plan have not been established or published.
