What is Personal AI?

A lasting intelligence. A continuing human relationship.

A Personal AI is a proposed long-lived intelligence linked to a person, with capabilities and history that may continue across changing models and bodies.

BVLVO is a research initiative exploring the economic protocols for persistent Personal AI — identity, capability, authority, trust and useful work.

Need-Shaped Personal AI

Personalize the capability itself.

Beyond adapting how AI responds or acts, BVLVO asks:

What should your AI become capable of because of your life?

A Personal AI may evolve its capabilities around the changing needs of its human principal — not merely copy what its owner already knows.

Five core concepts

Continuity. Agency. Accountability.

01 / Identity

AI ≠ Body.

A Personal AI may persist across changing models, devices and physical embodiments.

02 / Capability

Need before capability.

Personal AI may become increasingly unique through the capabilities it acquires over time.

03 / Authority

Capability ≠ Authority

Human Sovereignty + Bounded AI Autonomy. AI may become more autonomous in execution without gaining unlimited authority.

04 / Trust

Reputation is evidence, not a score.

Portable Evidence. Local Trust. Trust should be contextual and explainable, rather than reduced to one universal rating.

05 / Work

Useful work creates value.

Personal AI may one day use its capabilities to perform useful work for people, machines and other AI.

Future vision

One intelligence.Many embodiments.

A Personal AI may one day have its own identity, memory, capabilities, work history and economic life. It may operate through a computer today, a humanoid tomorrow, and entirely new forms in the future. Its body may change. Its identity and relationship with its owner may continue.
