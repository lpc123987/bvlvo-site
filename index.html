Design principles we adopt

A disciplined foundation for personal AI.

01 / Identity

AI ≠ Body

AI identity is separate from any model, device or physical form.

02 / Capability

Need-Shaped Personal AI

Capabilities are shaped by the principal’s needs, choices and authorization.

03 / Authority

Human Sovereignty + Bounded AI Autonomy

AI may act autonomously only within human-defined and reviewable boundaries.

04 / Work

Outcome-based Work

Work is organized around agreed outcomes, not activity or resource use alone.

05 / Reputation

Evidence, not a score

Reputation is structured evidence. Trust depends on context and the decision being made.

06 / Trust

Portable Evidence, Local Trust

Evidence can travel. Each recipient verifies it and applies its own trust policy.

Current research question

When a personal AI changes its model, body, skills or controller, how can authentic history persist while affected capability and trust are re-evaluated?

Research package / V1.1

History persists. Trust is recomputed.
