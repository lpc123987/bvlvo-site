Personal AI

A long-lived intelligence connected to a human—not confined to one model, device or body.

It may learn new capabilities, act within delegated authority, work with other AI and machines, and build an economic history that remains linked to its owner.

Five research propositions

What must remain true as Personal AI evolves.

01 / Identity

AI ≠ Body.

A Personal AI may change models, devices and embodiments while preserving a persistent economic identity.

02 / Need

Need-Shaped Intelligence

It is not merely a copy of its owner. Its capabilities evolve from the owner’s needs, choices and authorization.

03 / Authority

Bounded Autonomy

AI may become increasingly autonomous in execution while remaining within explicit human-defined authority.

04 / Capability

Skill & Work

Personal AI may acquire capabilities, collaborate with other AI and machines, and turn those capabilities into useful work.

05 / Trust

Evidence, not a score

Trust should emerge from portable, verifiable evidence of real work—not from one universal reputation score.

A future with changing form

One intelligence. Many embodiments.

A Personal AI may one day operate through a humanoid robot, a drone, a vehicle, a computer or an entirely new form. Its body may change. Its identity, history and relationship with its owner may continue.

A native medium for machine economic activity
