# BVLVO Task 001 — operator checklist

Protocol: task-001-v1. Initial budget: 1,000 non-monetary test points. Awarded: 0. Reserved: 0. No token conversion or airdrop entitlement.

## Intake

Public requests arrive in https://github.com/lpc123987/bvlvo-site/issues with [BVLVO-001] in the title. The website does not collect private contact information. GitHub account and attachments are public; contributors must not include secrets or private customer data.

## Reserve work before it starts

Confirm scope and a numbered slot in the issue before a contributor starts work expecting points. Caps: full experiment 2 × 200; independent review 3 × 100; distinct reproducible correction 2 × 100; assigned appeal review 1 × 100. Record reservations publicly and keep the two language pages synchronized. Unconfirmed requests reserve no points. Unused points remain unallocated. Do not retroactively lower an accepted slot's award.

## Acceptance

Full package: seeds 7, 42, 2026 × normal, shortage, outage × price, fair, spread = 27 runs. Include WORLD version, original exports, reproduction steps, one trade-off and one limitation. Team-generated reference results are examples, not independent submissions. Independent reviewers must disclose affiliations and cannot approve their own contribution. A review can disprove the original result and still qualify.

## Record decisions

For every item record issue URL, contributor, role, reserved points, reviewer, decision with reasons, date and awarded points. Deduplicate related submissions. Total awarded + reserved must never exceed 1,000 or the relevant pool cap. Unfilled positions stay unfilled. Publish no invented results or identities.

## Appeals and privacy

Assign appeals to a different reviewer. Do not penalize disagreement. If private data appears, do not copy it into the website; contact the submitter through the existing issue process and follow GitHub's removal procedures. Do not publish a person’s email on the ledger.

## End-of-round report

Publish real submissions, completed independent reviews, review time per package, unresolved disagreements and repeat-participation interest. If there are no external participants, call the exercise an internal rehearsal. Set the next deadline only when the operator commits to it, and announce changes publicly.
