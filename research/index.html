Paper 01

Principal-Need-Shaped Personal AI

Longitudinal Capability Evolution Around Human Needs

Formal Research / Benchmark Design

Most personalization systems adapt what AI says or does. This research asks whether personalization should extend to what a Personal AI becomes capable of over time. A Personal AI may evolve around its principal’s changing needs rather than merely mirror the principal’s existing skills.

Research Notes / adopted design principles

Constraints for the work ahead.

  • AI ≠ Body
  • Capability ≠ Authority
  • Reputation is evidence, not a score
  • Human Sovereignty + Bounded AI Autonomy
  • Need before capability
  • Useful work creates value

The Personal AI should remember more. The network should learn less.

These are principles we adopt, not claims that every principle originated with BVLVO. A principle is not an experimental result. Related work can overlap with individual ideas without settling the originality of the complete proposal; a limited search without precedent does not establish originality.
