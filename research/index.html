A research baseline for an open economic protocol connecting persistent identity, human authorization, skills, embodiment, useful work, verification, evidence, trust and economic coordination.

Current focus

Continuity without blind inheritance.

When a personal AI changes its model, body, skills or controller, how can authentic history persist while affected capability and trust are re-evaluated?

The leading research thread joins Reputation & Machine Trust Graph with Identity & Continuity. Deterministic authorization, work and verification are engineering prerequisites—not evidence that the main question has been solved.

The framework records adopted design constraints, hypotheses and open questions. An adopted principle is not an originality claim. A related work does not make the complete system non-original. Failure to find a precedent does not establish originality.

Research record

What changed.

Research V1

Direction reset

BVLVO moved from token-first planning to a Personal AI Economy / Machine Labor Economy research framework. Thirteen paper drafts were organized around design constraints, hypotheses, open questions and external evidence. Native-asset necessity remained open.

V1.1 / Papers V0.2

Evidence and originality review

The revision added claim-level prior-work judgments, stronger baselines and narrower language. It corrected overgeneralizations about AIP, cross-embodiment learning and cumulative delegation budgets, and focused the main research thread on evidence applicability after configuration change.

Prototype V0.1

Proposed, not executed

The planned prototype uses synthetic data, ordinary processes and simulated settlement. It has not been implemented or run. There are no performance results, production security claims, completed legal review or compatibility certification.
