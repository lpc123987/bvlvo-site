Current question

Continuity without blind inheritance.

When a Personal AI changes its model, body, skills or controller, how can authentic history persist while affected capability and trust are re-evaluated?

This is the current center of inquiry. The first systematic research round established a clearer problem map; prior-art review, papers, experiments, simulations and prototype validation remain ahead.

Adopted principles are design constraints, not proof of originality or implementation. Related work may overlap with individual mechanisms without resolving the complete problem. An absence found in a limited search is not evidence of originality.

Selected principles

Constraints that guide the research.

AI ≠ Body

Identity should not be reduced to a single model, device or embodiment.

Capability ≠ Authority

Being able to act does not mean being permitted to act.

Human Sovereignty + Bounded AI Autonomy

Execution may become autonomous while authority remains explicitly bounded by people.

Reputation is evidence, not a score

Evidence may travel. Trust remains a local decision made for a specific context.

Private memory, limited disclosure

The Personal AI should remember more. The network should learn less.

Useful work creates value

Economic value should follow verifiable contribution to real outcomes.

Publication approach

Release evidence at the pace it becomes defensible.
