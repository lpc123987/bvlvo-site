04 / FACTORY TRANSACTION

Prepare the atomic deployment

The constructor creates the token, team vesting and five reserve vaults, then allocates the complete supply. No token should remain with the factory or connected wallet.

The deployment button triggers a MetaMask confirmation. Check the network, sender, zero value and gas estimate there before signing. Closing or rejecting MetaMask sends nothing.
